description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=Are you using mods like KI5 vehicles or Vanilla Vehicles Animated?
description=Are you annoyed by animated vehicle parts collapsing into a broken mess at the center of your car?
description=Tired of coming back to your base or parking lot only to find doors, hoods, and custom armor pieces from SVU floating at coordinate (0,0,0) of the vehicle because the engine was left running?
description=
description=Thanks to this mod, Java bytecode instrumentation fixes this engine-level bug so your animated vehicles remain perfectly intact when reloading chunks!
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2][list]
description=[*] [b]Animated Vehicle Fix:[/b] Fixes the bug where vehicle parts reset and misalign to the center of the vehicle after leaving and returning to an active chunk while the engine is running.
description=[*] [b]Full SVU Support:[/b] Full compatibility with [i]Standardized Vehicle Upgrades[/i] (SVU) — custom armor plates, scoops, and window reinforcements remain properly attached.
description=[*] [b]Dynamic Model & Inventory Checks:[/b] Ensures uninstalled or missing parts do not ghost-render on your vehicles.
description=[*] [b]Light Beams:[/b] Headlight light cones (and tail lights) now responding after reloading the chunk.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url]:[/b] Since the bug is specific to the game's Java engine, ZombieBuddy is required.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer Compatibility[/h2]
description=This bug is strictly exclusive to [b]Singleplayer[/b] mode. If you are playing on a Multiplayer server (Hosted or Dedicated), this mod is [b]not required[/b] as the issue does not occur in MP.
